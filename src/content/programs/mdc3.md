MDC3 is Money Door FX Academy's most intensive program — a 12-month, one-to-one mentorship for students who've built a foundation through MDC1/MDC2 (or already trade professionally) and want to go further: institutional order-flow concepts, multi-timeframe analysis, and the portfolio-management discipline of a professional desk.

Because this program is built around direct mentorship rather than group sessions, pace and focus areas are adapted to each student — including dedicated preparation for funded-account (prop firm) evaluations for students pursuing that path.

This is a long-term commitment for students who are serious about trading as a skill worth mastering over years, not weeks.
