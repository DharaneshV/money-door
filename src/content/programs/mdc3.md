MDC3 is Money Door FX Academy's most intensive program — a 12-month, one-to-one mentorship for students who want to trade professionally, manage larger capital, pass prop firm evaluations, and build long-term consistency. This is not just another trading course; it is a complete trader transformation program built on everything taught in MDC2, plus a level of individual mentorship group sessions can't provide.

Because this program is built around direct mentorship rather than group sessions, pace and focus areas are adapted to each student — institutional order-flow concepts, multi-timeframe analysis, master-account and multi-account execution, and the portfolio-management discipline of a professional desk — including dedicated, phase-by-phase preparation for funded-account (prop firm) evaluations for students pursuing that path.

This is a long-term commitment for students who are serious about trading as a skill worth mastering over years, not weeks.
